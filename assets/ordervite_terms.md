Dear Supplier

Before you create the order make sure to check the following 

- Creating an order requires four entries 

1 – The order delivery destination

2- The order size (small, medium, large) 

3- The order money collection type (Cash, Transfer) 

4- The order price. 

All four entries listed above are required to create the order, once they are all set, click confirm and the order will be sent to our shippers who will do the delivery process 

Notes: 

- The order has six statuses two of them are controlled by the shipper and three by the shipper as following

1- NEW: when it is created and confirmed.

2- CONFIRMED: when one of our shippers confirm that he/she will perform the delivery process.

3- RECEIVED: when the shipper reaches to the supplier and confirm picking up the package.

4- DELIVERED: when the shipper delivers the order to its destination.

5- COMPLETE: when the supplier confirms the order has been done and he received the money amount from the shipper if it is mentioned to be cash type in the order entries or via money transfer means between the supplier and the package destination side.
 
6- CANCELED: The order has been canceled by the shipper or the supplier, and with that a fine may apply on the side who canceled according to the terms and conditions agreement with orderVITE.
